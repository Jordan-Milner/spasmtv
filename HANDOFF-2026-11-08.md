# Handoff — 2026-11-08 — emote saturation fixed, measured, shipped

Item: pulse `twitch-sim-emotes` (chat sim renders an emote on 3.3–5.7% of lines
in the rooms we imitate; the law is ≤10%, emote-only ≤4%).

## TL;DR

The 10-min harness put a **rendered emote on 42.7% of lines** (baseline,
`/tmp/pulse-emotes-before.jsonl`, chat 253). The worker-prompt gate from
`c612dff` was never the problem — model lines carried codes at 8% in the
baseline log. The saturation was the **scheduler's emote-kind machinery**: the
template `emote` kind was 43% of all lines and bore a code 90% of the time,
plus chants (0.5/0.35) and a 5% dress append.

Fix (all scheduler/template land, no prompt or model change):
`KIND_WEIGHTS.emote` 18→10, the kind's coded split 85%→12%, chants 0.5/0.35→
0.08/0.03, `pileOnKind` 0.55→0.75 (waves stay free-line-heavy for the gen
budget), dress 5%→2%, `TEXT_FILLS` mined from `data/real/` to 26 strings.

**Final measured** (`/tmp/pulse-emotes-after2.jsonl`, chat 232): emote
**6.9% PASS**, emote-only **2.6% PASS**, repeat 5.2% (real 4.7, MATCH),
sceneLinkage 10.6% PASS, burst 2.27×, gen budget 19.7 calls/min ≤ 20.
Shipped: `3882bc9` on master, Pages built from it, https://spasm.milner.zone/ live.

Full lab log with both iterations (iteration 1 traded the saturation for a
36% repeat wall — fixed by corpus-mining the fills): `notes/PULSE-EMOTES-2026-11.md`.

## What to know for the next chat-quality item

- **Names/reply rate is the remaining law mismatch**: sim 17.7% vs real 9.1/7.7
  (rubric bands want 4–11% / 6–8%). It read 13.8/13.8 at baseline — already
  failing, and untouched by this item (`threadSeed`, `fireThreadReply` are
  untouched; ~1.2pp of the rise is the smaller denominator). Tuning it means
  cutting the thread seed rate, which buys the band by spending the
  "chat talks to itself" live-feel texture — needs its own measured pass.
- Hairline rubric FAILs, all within run swing (baseline in parens): one-word
  36.6 (32, ceiling 36 — one line over), ALL-CAPS 9.1 (6.3, floor 10 — moved
  toward the band and MATCHes real within tol), exclamation 3.02 (1.2, n=7
  vs n=3), question 15.9 (13.4, pre-existing FAIL but compare-MATCH).
- **`TEXT_FILLS` (js/cadence.js) is a measured pool now** — every string is
  corpus-evidenced or a cultural staple; don't hand-add vibes. One-room memes
  ("aura"×194, "d3"×187) and loaded-code tokens ("kek", "icant") were
  deliberately excluded: the former is a stranger's inside joke, the latter
  renders as an image.
- The new cadence pins in `tools/test-cadence.mjs` fail loudly if the coded
  share creeps back (≤20% of the kind's lines, chant rates, weight ≤12).
- **The gen budget is the binding constraint the emote share services**: the
  pile-on sim gate (≤20 generate calls/min) caught `pileOnKind` 0.70. If you
  trim the free share again, the queue backs up during scene waves and chat
  goes slow-and-static — tune `templateLine`'s coded split, not the weight.
